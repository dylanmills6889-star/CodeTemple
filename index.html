How it works

  • The game teaches two things. A word is any piece of code or Unity talk, explained in plain words. Example: static, or Inspector. A line is a full line of code you use a lot. Example: void Update().
  • Every word and every line comes with an example. A picture when one is possible, a written example when not. Example: the word Inspector shows a drawn Inspector with one row lit in gold. Gold always means look here.
  • Before a new line shows up, you learn every word in it. Example: before using UnityEngine; you learn script, using, UnityEngine, and the semicolon.
  • A word takes two steps. First you pick what it means. Then you bring the word back from its meaning: code words get typed, ideas and symbols get picked. Example: you see void and pick its meaning. Later you see the meaning and type void.
  • A line takes three steps. Pick it from four choices, type its missing piece, then type the whole line from memory. The third time you type it right, its stone goes on the pyramid. Example: for private Rigidbody2D rb; the missing piece is Rigidbody2D.
  • Every new line comes with a piece by piece breakdown and a small script that shows where the line goes, with the line lit in its spot.
  • Tap any word with a dotted underline to see what it means, with its example. Try it here: Inspector.
  • A right answer moves a card up a step. A miss moves it down a step and brings it back sooner. Example: miss a line on the typing step and you get its missing piece question again 2 cards later.
  • Finished cards come back for one review after an hour, then a day, 3 days, a week, 2 weeks, and a month. Miss a review and you have to win the card back.
  • Right answers in a row build a run. Example: at 3 in a row a flame lights up next to the run counter, and every right answer chimes a little higher than the one before.
  • The dots on a result show how far that card is. Example: a line has 5 dots. When all 5 are full, its stone drops onto the pyramid.
  • Sound can be switched off on the home screen. It also stays quiet while the iPhone is set to silent.
  • Capital letters count, because C# cares about them. Spaces do not count. Example: Transform is a type and transform is the Transform of this object. Two different things.
  • A few short sessions on different days beat one long session. Example: 15 minutes tonight, 15 tomorrow, then the plane.
